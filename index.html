About me

I got into analytics through the side door: customer support.

I joined Simfoni in February 2026 answering tickets from people who couldn't make sense of their spend reports. It turned out to be the best training I could have had, because I learned what people actually ask of their data before I ever opened Power BI for work.

Since May I've been on the data analytics team. I look after four Power BI dashboards for multiple clients, and I spend a good chunk of every week fixing supplier records that were filed under the wrong category. It's unglamorous and it matters more than any chart, because a dashboard is only as honest as the data under it.

Excel is open on my screen every day, for cleaning and classifying client data, and I check my SQL in Snowflake before anything ships. I'd rather send a client one clear number than twelve confusing ones.

Right now: Building dashboards, fixing taxonomies, and learning more about pipelines and analytics engineering. Ask me about spend data.
